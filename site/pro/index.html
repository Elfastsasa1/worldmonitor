Frequently asked questions about World Monitor Pro

Is World Monitor still free?
Yes. The core platform remains free. Pro adds the AI analyst, the personal intelligence digest, MCP connectors, and the widget builder.
What is MCP?
MCP lets AI agents — Claude, GPT, custom LLMs — use World Monitor as a tool, querying live services. Included in Pro.
How personalized is the digest?
Pick your cadence — daily, twice-daily, or weekly. We re-score every tracked story against your alert rules and watchlist, dedupe across sources, and send up to 30 ranked items per digest with an AI Assessment and Signals to watch. Real-time alerts are also available for critical events.
