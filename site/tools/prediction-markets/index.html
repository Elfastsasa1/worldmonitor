How to read a prediction market price

Each market is a yes-or-no question that pays out if the event happens. The price of a "yes" share, between 0 and 100 cents, is what traders currently pay for it, and is commonly read as the market's probability of the event. Thinly traded markets can move on small orders, so volume is shown beside each price.
