How to monitor the situation

  1. Open the live map. The map and its panels are free and need no account.
  2. Pick a situation above and open its tracker page, or the map view it links to, for the event-level detail behind the monthly figure.
  3. Open a country page for that country's instability and resilience scores and its recent reporting.
  4. Set an alert rule on a paid plan. Rules filter by event type, severity and country, deliver to Slack, Microsoft Teams, Discord, Telegram, email, browser push or a signed webhook, and run in real time or as a daily, twice-daily or weekly digest. See the notifications reference.
  5. Connect an agent. The MCP server, the REST API, the Python, Go and Ruby SDKs and the npm CLI read the same data; setup steps are on the agent page.

What does "monitor the situation" mean?

To monitor the situation is to keep watching a developing event, usually a conflict, a disaster or a market shock, as new reports arrive. The phrase spread online as a joke about people who refresh the news constantly without being able to change anything. This page is the version with sources: every reading above names where it came from and when it was taken, and nothing on it is a forecast.
